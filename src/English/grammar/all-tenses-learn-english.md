# 用一句话串完英语时态：I / she / they learn English

时态难，往往不是「公式记不住」，而是**每一格到底在说哪一种时间视角**。

本文只用一组主语和同一个动作：

- **I** learn English  
- **she** learn**s** English（第三人称单数，一般现在要加 **-s**）  
- **they** learn English  

把常见的 **12 种核心时态**全部走一遍。拼写按美式：**learned**（英式常见 *learnt*，意思相同）。

> 读法建议：先看「人话」，再看三行例句，最后扫总表。不必一次背完。

---

## 先认两把尺子

英语时态 ≈ **时间** × **体（动作形态）**：

| 时间 | 体：一般 | 体：进行 | 体：完成 | 体：完成进行 |
|------|----------|----------|----------|--------------|
| **现在** | 习惯 / 事实 | 此刻正在 | 到现在已完成（或有结果） | 持续到现在，还在强调过程 |
| **过去** | 过去某时发生 | 过去某刻正在 | 过去某时之前已完成 | 过去某时之前一直在做 |
| **将来** | 将要发生 | 将来某刻正在 | 将来某时之前会完成 | 将来某时之前会一直在做 |

后面 12 节，就是这张表的 12 格。

---

## 1. 一般现在时 Present Simple

**在说什么：** 习惯、常态、事实——不是「此刻这一秒」。

- I **learn** English every day.  
- She **learns** English every day.  
- They **learn** English every day.

**何时用：** 课表、习惯、真理；时间词常见 *every day / usually / often*。

---

## 2. 现在进行时 Present Continuous

**在说什么：** 说话时正在发生，或当前阶段在做的事。

- I **am learning** English (right now / these days).  
- She **is learning** English (right now / these days).  
- They **are learning** English (right now / these days).

**何时用：** *now / at the moment / these days*；临时安排也可。

---

## 3. 现在完成时 Present Perfect

**在说什么：** 从过去某点到**现在**——动作已发生，重点常在**对现在的影响**或**经历**，不一定说清「哪一天」。

- I **have learned** English for three years. / I **have learned** a lot.  
- She **has learned** English for three years. / She **has learned** a lot.  
- They **have learned** English for three years. / They **have learned** a lot.

**何时用：** *already / yet / ever / never / for / since*；「到现在为止」。

---

## 4. 现在完成进行时 Present Perfect Continuous

**在说什么：** 过去开始，**一直持续到现在**，强调「过程还在继续」或「一直在做」。

- I **have been learning** English for three years.  
- She **has been learning** English for three years.  
- They **have been learning** English for three years.

**何时用：** 和现在完成常可互换；要突出「一直学着、还没停」时更贴这格。

---

## 5. 一般过去时 Past Simple

**在说什么：** 过去某个时间点/段发生，**已经结束**，常带明确过去时间。

- I **learned** English in 2020.  
- She **learned** English in 2020.  
- They **learned** English in 2020.

**何时用：** *yesterday / last year / in 2020 / ago*。

---

## 6. 过去进行时 Past Continuous

**在说什么：** 过去某一时刻**正在**进行；常作「背景」，另一件事打断它。

- I **was learning** English at 8 p.m. yesterday.  
- She **was learning** English at 8 p.m. yesterday.  
- They **were learning** English at 8 p.m. yesterday.

**何时用：** *at 8 p.m. / when you called* ——「那时正在……」。

---

## 7. 过去完成时 Past Perfect

**在说什么：** 在**过去某一时刻之前**已经完成（「过去的过去」）。

- I **had learned** English before I moved abroad.  
- She **had learned** English before she moved abroad.  
- They **had learned** English before they moved abroad.

**何时用：** 两个过去动作排先后：先发生的用过去完成，后发生的用一般过去。

---

## 8. 过去完成进行时 Past Perfect Continuous

**在说什么：** 在过去某时刻之前，**一直持续**做某事（强调过程）。

- I **had been learning** English for two years before the exam.  
- She **had been learning** English for two years before the exam.  
- They **had been learning** English for two years before the exam.

**何时用：** 「到那时为止，已经学了两年」——突出持续时间。

---

## 9. 一般将来时 Future Simple（will）

**在说什么：** 将来会发生；常带决定、预测、承诺。

- I **will learn** English next year.  
- She **will learn** English next year.  
- They **will learn** English next year.

**何时用：** *tomorrow / next year / I think…*。

### 附注：be going to（不算第 13 格，但很常用）

已有计划/迹象时，口语里常用 **be going to**：

- I **am going to learn** English this summer.  
- She **is going to learn** English this summer.  
- They **are going to learn** English this summer.

和 *will* 常可互换；语感上 *going to* 更「计划已定 / 迹象明显」，*will* 更「当场决定 / 单纯预测」。

---

## 10. 将来进行时 Future Continuous

**在说什么：** 将来某一时刻**正在**进行。

- I **will be learning** English at 9 a.m. tomorrow.  
- She **will be learning** English at 9 a.m. tomorrow.  
- They **will be learning** English at 9 a.m. tomorrow.

**何时用：** 「明天九点我（们）会正在学英语」。

---

## 11. 将来完成时 Future Perfect

**在说什么：** 到将来某一时刻**之前**会完成。

- I **will have learned** English by next June.  
- She **will have learned** English by next June.  
- They **will have learned** English by next June.

（若语境是「学完某个阶段」，也可说 *will have finished learning…*；这里仍用同一动词骨架便于对照。）

**何时用：** *by then / by next year* ——「到那时已经……」。

---

## 12. 将来完成进行时 Future Perfect Continuous

**在说什么：** 到将来某时刻为止，**已经持续做了多久**（强调过程）。

- By next June, I **will have been learning** English for three years.  
- By next June, she **will have been learning** English for three years.  
- By next June, they **will have been learning** English for three years.

**何时用：** 「到明年六月，就已经学了三年了」。

---

## 总表：12 格一览（I / she / they）

| 时态 | I | she | they |
|------|---|-----|------|
| 一般现在 | I learn English. | She learns English. | They learn English. |
| 现在进行 | I am learning English. | She is learning English. | They are learning English. |
| 现在完成 | I have learned English. | She has learned English. | They have learned English. |
| 现在完成进行 | I have been learning English. | She has been learning English. | They have been learning English. |
| 一般过去 | I learned English. | She learned English. | They learned English. |
| 过去进行 | I was learning English. | She was learning English. | They were learning English. |
| 过去完成 | I had learned English. | She had learned English. | They had learned English. |
| 过去完成进行 | I had been learning English. | She had been learning English. | They had been learning English. |
| 一般将来 | I will learn English. | She will learn English. | They will learn English. |
| 将来进行 | I will be learning English. | She will be learning English. | They will be learning English. |
| 将来完成 | I will have learned English. | She will have learned English. | They will have learned English. |
| 将来完成进行 | I will have been learning English. | She will have been learning English. | They will have been learning English. |

第三人称单数记住三处易错：**learns**、**is**、**has**（不是 *learn / are / have*）。

---

## 三组最容易混的

**一般现在 vs 现在进行**  
- *She learns English every day.*（习惯）  
- *She is learning English right now.*（此刻）

**一般过去 vs 现在完成**  
- *They learned English in 2020.*（过去时间说死了）  
- *They have learned English.*（到现在为止有这段经历/结果，未必说年份）

**will vs be going to**  
- *I will learn English.*（预测 / 当场表态）  
- *I am going to learn English this summer.*（计划已有）

---

## 收束

**你现在会了：** 时态 = 时间（现/过/将）× 体（一般/进行/完成/完成进行）；同一句 *learn English* 可以填满 12 格；*she* 要盯三人称变位。

**刻意没展开：** 虚拟语气、情态动词全表、被动全套——需要时可另开一篇，仍可用 *learn English* 做锚点。

复习时：合上讲解，只看总表，随机抽一格口头说出 I / she / they 三句。
